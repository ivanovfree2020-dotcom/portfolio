Quebec City: History & Landmarks

Explore the historic heart of North America’s only fortified city north of Mexico.

The Cradle of New France

Established in 1608 by explorer Samuel de Champlain, Quebec City boasts a rich military, political, and cultural heritage. Its historic district (Old Quebec) is a UNESCO World Heritage treasure enclosed by centuries-old stone walls, featuring European-style cobblestone streets, iconic architecture, and breathtaking views of the St. Lawrence River.

Château Frontenac

Perched high on Cape Diamond, this majestic grand railway hotel is widely considered the most photographed hotel in the entire world, defining the city's iconic skyline.

The Citadelle of Québec

An active military installation and North America's oldest British-style fortress, shaped like a giant star and guarding the strategic heights of the city.

Place Royale & Petit Champlain

The historic birthplace of French civilization on the continent, filled with 17th-century stone houses, artisan boutiques, and charming pedestrian alleyways.
